# Contributing

- Keep bench prototypes on standard `XT60`, `USB-C PD`, `4-pin JST-GH / Grove I2C`, and `UART` headers.
- Keep all images in root `images/` named `device_subject_detail_number.jpg` (~1600 px wide, JPEG q=80, zero GPS/EXIF).
- Do not flip `- [ ]` to `- [x]` or guess unverified measurements.
